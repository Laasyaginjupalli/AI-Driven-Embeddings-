# Predictive & Prescriptive Analytics for Business Decision Making

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing customer churn for TechSaaS Solutions using predictive and prescriptive analytics. It uses customer data to identify accounts at risk of leaving, understand the key drivers of churn, forecast future revenue, and evaluate the effectiveness of retention campaigns. The findings support data-driven decisions to improve customer retention and protect business revenue.

## Objectives

- Identify customers who are likely to churn using predictive modelling.
- Analyse the key factors influencing customer attrition.
- Forecast future revenue based on existing churn trends.
- Evaluate the effectiveness of customer retention campaigns.
- Develop targeted strategies to improve customer engagement and retention.

## Methodologies & Frameworks

- LightGBM – Customer Churn Prediction
- Temporal Fusion Transformer (TFT) – Revenue Forecasting
- SHAP – Explainable AI and Churn Driver Analysis
- EconML – Causal Inference and Treatment Effect Analysis
- Model Evaluation using AUC, Accuracy and MAE
- Average Treatment Effect (ATE) and Conditional Average Treatment Effect (CATE)

## Analytics Applications

- Customer Churn Prediction
- Revenue Forecasting
- Customer Behaviour and Engagement Analysis
- Churn Driver Identification
- Retention Campaign Effectiveness
- Customer Segmentation
- Data-Driven Business Decision Making

## Key Findings

- LightGBM achieved 72.04% accuracy and an AUC score of 0.7495 in predicting customer churn.
- The TFT model forecasted six months of revenue with an MAE of 55.22.
- SHAP analysis identified low product usage, poor feature adoption, payment delays, support ticket frequency and lack of training participation as major churn drivers.
- Causal analysis showed that retention campaigns improved retention by an average of 1.34%.
- Highly engaged customers responded 64% better to retention campaigns than customers with lower engagement.

## Recommendations

- Integrate churn prediction alerts into CRM systems to support proactive customer outreach.
- Target retention campaigns towards customers most likely to respond.
- Improve product engagement through training, webinars and onboarding support.
- Strengthen support services for enterprise customers.
- Introduce flexible billing options and payment reminders to reduce payment delays.
